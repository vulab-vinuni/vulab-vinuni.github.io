      Batteries and electrolytes

      We develop nonflammable and solvent-free electrolytes, including low-melting molten salts and fluorinated ethers, for lithium-metal and fast-charging batteries, and apply data-driven approaches to electrolyte discovery.

      • Electrolytes for lithium-metal and all-temperature fast charging
      • Solid-state and polymer electrolytes
      • Battery safety and recycling; additive manufacturing of energy storage devices
      Li-metal batteriesElectrolytesBattery safetyRecycling

      Thermal management composites

      Electronics fail when heat cannot escape. We build bioinspired papers, films and polymer composites from graphene fluoride, aramid nanofibers, aligned SiC and MXene that conduct heat efficiently while staying electrically insulating and nonflammable.

      • Nacre-inspired and vertically aligned filler architectures
      • Flame-retardant, fire-safe multifunctional materials
      Thermal conductivityPolymer nanocompositesFlame retardancy

      EMI shielding and flexible electronics

      Lightweight composites that block electromagnetic interference while managing heat, and flexible, wearable and printed devices for next-generation electronics.

      • MXene and graphene hybrid shielding composites
      • Printed and wearable energy and electronic devices
      EMI shieldingMXenePrinted electronics

      High-entropy nanomaterials for energy and electronics New direction

      Mixing five or more principal elements on one sublattice stabilizes phases that simple compounds cannot reach and opens new electrochemical, catalytic and electronic behavior. We synthesize high-entropy oxides, alloys and chalcogenides and use DFT, special quasirandom structures and machine-learned interatomic potentials to understand and predict them.

      • Composition-property maps for electrodes and electrocatalysts
      • Phase stability, ion diffusion and electronic structure from first principles
      • Ongoing project: High Entropy Chemistries for Energy and Electronics
      High-entropy oxidesDFT / SQSML potentialsNanomaterials

          Join us

          We welcome materials scientists, chemists, physicists and engineers, experimental and computational.

          Postdoctoral researchers

          • PhD in materials science, chemistry, physics or chemical engineering
          • Experience in batteries, composites or high-entropy materials

          Research assistants

          • Synthesis and electrochemical testing
          • DFT and machine-learning modelling
          • Good fit before PhD study abroad

          Undergraduates and interns

          • VinUniversity students in any engineering or science major
          • Visiting students and summer interns

          How to apply

          1. Read our recent papers and pick the research direction you want to work on.
          2. Email the PI at canh.vm@vinuni.edu.vn with your CV, transcripts and a one-paragraph statement of interest. Use the subject line “Prospective member – [your name]”.
          3. Shortlisted applicants are invited for an interview and a short presentation on previous work.
